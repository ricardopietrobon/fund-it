# Codebook

Hybrid frame: deductive codes derived from the three hypotheses and the eight guide domains,
plus inductive codes for material that did not fit. Codes were compared across cases with
sites ordered by national rank, to locate convergence and divergence.

## Deductive codes

| Code | Hypothesis / domain | Definition | Observed |
|---|---|---|---|
| `HIRE-SENIOR` | H1 / domain 2 | Recruiting established investigators with existing portfolios as a growth strategy | 1 of 5 sites (Lexington) |
| `HIRE-EARLY` | inductive counterpart to H1 | Recruiting early-career investigators as the primary growth strategy | Bedford, Minneapolis; Bedford states senior recruitment is explicitly not the strategy, because such investigators prefer the larger nearby academic center |
| `ALLOC-CONCENTRATE` | H2 / domain 3 | Internal funds directed to a few productive laboratories | 0 of 5 sites |
| `ALLOC-DISTRIBUTE` | inductive counterpart to H2 | Internal funds distributed by individual productivity; struggling laboratories given temporary support to prepare a resubmission rather than defunded | Bedford explicitly; Gainesville and Minneapolis describe bridge and pilot funds used the same stabilizing way |
| `FOCUS-DEPTH` | H3 / domain 4 | Deliberate concentration in a small number of research areas | 3 of 5 sites — the one hypothesis the interviews supported |

## Inductive codes

| Code | Definition | Observed |
|---|---|---|
| `PROTECTED-TIME` | Research time defended at the level of the medical center director rather than within the research service | 4 of 5 sites; only Lexington did not raise it |
| `RESEARCH-ADMIN` | A staffed research administration office as a precondition for investigator recruitment and retention | 3 of 5 sites; most concretely at Gainesville, where the medical center pays roughly a dozen administrative salaries |
| `AFFILIATE` | The academic affiliate relationship as the mechanism for sharing faculty and trainees | 5 of 5 sites; named as the binding constraint at VA Pacific Islands, where competition rather than collaboration with the state university was described as a limit on growth |

## Coding procedure

Single analyst. No second coder and no member checking. Codes applied to structured
interview summaries, not verbatim transcripts, because the approved protocol requires
recordings to be deleted after transcription. Quotation in the sense qualitative researchers
mean it is therefore unavailable, and the Results report participants' positions in
reported speech.
