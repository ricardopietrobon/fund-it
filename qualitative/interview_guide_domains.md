# Interview guide — domains

Semi-structured interviews, 45–60 minutes, conducted remotely with Associate Chiefs of Staff
for Research and Development and executive directors of affiliated nonprofit corporations.

Each interview opened by presenting the site's own quantitative funding profile, built from
the same RePORTER extraction and sent with the invitation. Participants were then asked open
questions about how that profile had come about, which allowed them to confirm, correct or
reinterpret it before the domain questions began.

Eight domains, in order:

1. Role and context
2. Strategic hiring
3. Funding allocation
4. Research focus versus breadth
5. Institutional supports
6. Culture and leadership
7. Adaptation to a changing funding environment
8. Advice to a site seeking to improve

Domains 2, 3 and 4 correspond to hypotheses H1, H2 and H3 respectively.
