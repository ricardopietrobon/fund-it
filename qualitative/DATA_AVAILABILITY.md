# Qualitative data availability

Interview recordings are destroyed after transcription under USF IRB Study 009006, and
analysis used the structured summaries retained after deletion. Participating VA facilities
are identifiable from their publicly reported funding profiles, so neither the summaries nor
quotations can be deposited without breaking the confidentiality offered at consent.

The materials in this directory are the shareable substitute: the instrument, the coding
frame, and the rule by which the joint display was built. They let a reader judge how the
qualitative claims were produced even though the underlying text cannot be released.

Three limitations are not remediable by any artifact and are stated in the manuscript:
coding was performed by one analyst, no member checking was carried out, and thematic
saturation was not reached at five of a planned 18–20 interviews.

**Provenance note.** The files here are reconstructed from the manuscript's Method and
Results sections, which are the only surviving description of the instrument and coding
frame in the project archive. They should be checked against the analyst's working codebook
before deposit; where the two differ, the working codebook governs.
