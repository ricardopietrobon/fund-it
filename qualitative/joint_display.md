# Joint display — construction rule

Table 5 places each site's quantitative profile beside its reported strategy. One row per
site, sites ordered by national rank.

Quantitative columns, all regenerated by `scripts/01_site_profiles.py`:

- award total, FY2011–2024, VA-administered only
- I01 share (%) — the signature of a mature portfolio of independently funded investigators
- IK1+IK2 share (%) — career development
- growth ratio, FY2018–2024 over FY2011–2017

Qualitative columns, from the codebook: the hiring code, the allocation code and the focus
code assigned to that site, with the inductive codes that also applied.

Agreements and disagreements between the strands are read as findings rather than as error.
The display's reported content is: the strands agree on research focus, disagree on hiring,
and disagree on concentration of internal resources.

**Two cautions carried from the numeric audit** (REPRODUCIBILITY.md):

- Percentage columns for VA Pacific Islands rest on 7 awards and are not comparable with the
  other four sites. The manuscript says so; the display must not invite the comparison.
- The growth-ratio column as published was computed on a different award universe than the
  percentage columns in the same row (finding R-3), and for VA Pacific Islands the
  facility-only direction is opposite to the published one. Regenerate the column from
  `scripts/01_site_profiles.py` before the display is used again.
